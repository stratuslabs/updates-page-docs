# Cover Images

Cover images make your announcements more visually appealing and improve engagement when shared on social media.

## Adding a cover image

1. In the announcement editor, click the **image icon** in the header area
2. Upload an image or drag and drop
3. The image will appear as the announcement's cover

## Recommended specifications

| Property | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Dimensions** | 1200 x 630px (1.91:1 ratio) |
| **Format** | PNG or JPG |
| **File size** | Under 2MB |
| **Text** | Keep minimal — may be cropped |

<Tip>
  The 1200x630 size is optimized for social media sharing (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook).
</Tip>

## Where cover images appear

Cover images are displayed in:

- **Your changelog portal** — At the top of the announcement detail page
- **Social media previews** — When the announcement URL is shared
- **RSS feeds** — As the enclosure/media element
- **Embeds** — In widget and inline embeds (if enabled)

## Best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use consistent branding">
    Create a template with your brand colors and logo. This makes your changelog look professional and recognizable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Show the feature">
    Screenshots or mockups of the actual feature work great. Users can immediately see what changed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Avoid too much text">
    Social platforms may crop images differently. Keep text to a headline at most.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Consider dark mode">
    Test how your image looks on both light and dark backgrounds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Tools for creating cover images

Some tools that work great for changelog images:

- **[Figma](https://figma.com)** — Design custom images with templates
- **[Canva](https://canva.com)** — Easy drag-and-drop image creation
- **[CleanShot](https://cleanshot.com)** — Polished screenshots with annotations
- **[OG Image Generator](https://og-image.vercel.app)** — Auto-generate images from text
