# Scheduling Announcements

Plan ahead by scheduling announcements to publish automatically at a future date and time.

## How to schedule an announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your announcement">
    Write your announcement as usual in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the publish dropdown">
    Instead of clicking **Publish**, click the dropdown arrow next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Schedule'">
    Choose the date and time you want the announcement to go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Schedule** to confirm. Your announcement will publish automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing scheduled announcements

Scheduled announcements appear in your announcements list with a **Scheduled** badge.

You can:
- **Edit** the content before it publishes
- **Reschedule** to a different time
- **Publish now** to make it live immediately
- **Delete** to cancel the scheduled announcement

## Timezone handling

Scheduled times use your account's timezone setting. To change your timezone:

1. Go to **Settings → Account → General**
2. Select your timezone from the dropdown
3. Click **Save**

<Note>
  Changing your timezone won't affect already-scheduled announcements. They'll still publish at the originally intended moment.
</Note>
