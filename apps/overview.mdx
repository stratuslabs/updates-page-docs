# Apps

Apps connect your changelog to other tools. Today they do one job: when someone subscribes on your portal, we hand them straight to a tool of yours — your email provider, or an endpoint you run.

<Note>
  Apps are available on the **Pro** plan and above. [Collecting subscribers](/portal/subscriptions) works on every plan.
</Note>

## Connecting an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your subscription settings">
    Go to **Settings → Portal → Subscriptions** and find the app under **Apps**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect** and paste the API key from that tool. We check the key with the provider before saving it, so you find out immediately if it's wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where subscribers go">
    For an email app, pick the list, tag, or segment new subscribers should be added to. Webhook has no list — connecting it is the whole setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it. New subscribers reach the connected tool within seconds of signing up.

The **Webhook** app works a little differently: instead of an API key you give us a URL, and instead of picking a list we POST the whole event to you.

## How syncing works

We sync a subscriber when they sign up, and again if their status changes.

**Email apps** put them in the list, tag or segment you picked. When someone unsubscribes from your changelog we undo what we did — we take them out of that list, and no further: we don't unsubscribe them from your email tool altogether, because they may be on other lists of yours that have nothing to do with your changelog.

That's also why **choosing a list or tag isn't optional** for those apps. It's what lets us honour an opt-out later, so an email app that doesn't have one yet won't sync at all — it will say so on the Subscriptions page — rather than adding people we'd have no way to remove.

**[Webhook](/apps/webhook) has no list to choose, so none of that applies to it.** We POST the subscriber to your endpoint on every change, opt-outs and deletions included, and what happens next is your code's decision. It starts syncing the moment it's connected.

Syncing is one-way and it starts when you connect. Contacts you add directly in your email tool don't appear on your Subscribers page, and **nothing is sent retroactively** — not the people who subscribed before you connected the app, and not anyone who signed up while a connection was broken. Reconnecting resumes syncing from that moment; it doesn't go back and catch up.

Export is how you close that gap: the Subscribers page gives you the whole list as a CSV, to import into your email tool.

## When something goes wrong

Whatever the app, the Subscriptions page shows what went wrong.

For an **email app**, a rejected sync usually means the API key was revoked or regenerated: syncing stops and the card asks you to reconnect with a fresh key.

For **[Webhook](/apps/webhook)**, most failures don't stop the app: we keep delivering, and the error stays on the card until a delivery succeeds. What does stop it is your endpoint refusing us outright — `401` or `403`, which reads as your signature check rejecting us, and `410 Gone`, which says the endpoint is permanently retired. [The full table is on the webhook page.](/apps/webhook#how-we-handle-your-responses)

Your changelog is never affected by this. Subscribers are still saved to your account even when no app is connected or a connected app is failing, so you never lose sign-ups while you sort it out.

## Disconnecting

Open the **⋯** menu next to a connected app and choose **Disconnect**. This deletes the stored credential. It doesn't remove anyone from the list in your email tool, and it doesn't tell a webhook endpoint anything — we simply stop sending.

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my API key stored?">
    Encrypted, and never sent back to your browser — the settings page shows only the last four characters so you can tell which key is in use. The same is true of a webhook URL: it's a credential, so it isn't shown back to you either, and changing it means reconnecting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I downgrade to Free?">
    Syncing stops, and your connection is kept. Upgrade again and it resumes without you having to paste the key back in. Subscribers keep being collected the whole time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one app?">
    Yes. Each connected app gets its own copy of every new subscriber.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will there be non-email apps?">
    That's the plan — Apps is built to cover things like posting to Slack when you publish. The list above is what exists today.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
