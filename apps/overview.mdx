# Apps

Apps connect your changelog to other tools. Today they do one job: when someone subscribes on your portal, we add them to your email tool automatically.

<Note>
  Apps are available on the **Pro** plan and above. [Collecting subscribers](/portal/subscriptions) works on every plan.
</Note>

## Connecting an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your subscription settings">
    Go to **Settings → Portal → Subscriptions** and find the app under **Apps**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect** and paste the API key from that tool. We check the key with the provider before saving it, so you find out immediately if it's wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where subscribers go">
    Pick the list, tag, or segment new subscribers should be added to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it. New subscribers reach the connected tool within seconds of signing up.

## How syncing works

We sync a subscriber when they sign up, and again if their status changes.

When someone unsubscribes from your changelog, we undo what we did — we remove them from the list, tag, or segment we added them to. We don't unsubscribe them from your email tool altogether, because they may be on other lists of yours that have nothing to do with your changelog. **If you connected an app without choosing a list or tag, there's nothing for us to remove them from, so an unsubscribe won't reach your email tool at all.** Choose one if you want unsubscribes mirrored.

Syncing is one-way. Contacts you add directly in your email tool don't appear on your Subscribers page, and people who subscribed **before** you connected the app aren't sent retroactively — reconnecting the app backfills anyone added since it last synced.

## When something goes wrong

If your email tool rejects a sync — usually because the API key was revoked or regenerated — the app shows an error on the Subscriptions page with what went wrong, and stops trying. Reconnect with a fresh key to start it again.

Your changelog is never affected by this. Subscribers are still saved to your account even when no app is connected or a connected app is failing, so you never lose sign-ups while you sort it out.

## Disconnecting

Open the **⋯** menu next to a connected app and choose **Disconnect**. This deletes the stored credential; it doesn't remove anyone from your list in the other tool.

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my API key stored?">
    Encrypted, and never sent back to your browser — the settings page shows only the last four characters so you can tell which key is in use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I downgrade to Free?">
    Syncing stops, and your connection is kept. Upgrade again and it resumes without you having to paste the key back in. Subscribers keep being collected the whole time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one app?">
    Yes. Each connected app gets its own copy of every new subscriber.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will there be non-email apps?">
    That's the plan — Apps is built to cover things like posting to Slack when you publish. The list above is what exists today.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
