# Quickstart Guide

Get your changelog up and running in just a few steps.

## 1. Create your account

Head to [app.updates.page/signup](https://app.updates.page/signup) and create your account. A short setup walks you through:

- Your name and avatar
- Your company name, logo, and subdomain (e.g., `yourcompany.updates.page`)
- What you want to do first — we use this to suggest your next steps

## 2. Customize your portal

After signing up, you'll land in the dashboard. Let's make it yours:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a template">
    Go to **Settings → Portal → Customize** and choose Modern, Classic, or Cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your logo and colors">
    Upload a logo, then set your header and page colors. The preview updates as you edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a welcome message">
    Optionally add a title and description that appear at the top of your changelog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Create your first post

Click the **+** button in the top right of the Posts page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Writing">
    Use the rich text editor to write your update. You can:
    - Format text with **bold**, *italic*, and `code`
    - Add headings (H1, H2)
    - Create bullet and numbered lists
    - Insert images and videos
    - Add code blocks with syntax highlighting
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Metadata">
    Before publishing, set:
    - **Category** — New, Improvement, Fix, or create your own
    - **Author** — Who wrote this update
    - **Cover image** — Optional hero image for the post
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 4. Publish!

**Save draft** keeps your work without publishing. When you're ready, click **Publish** and choose **Now** — your post is live at your changelog URL.

<Note>
  Choose **Later** in the same dialog to [schedule](/posts/scheduling) the post for a future date.
</Note>

## 5. Share your changelog

Your changelog is live at:

```
https://yourcompany.updates.page
```

Share this link with your users, or:
- [Set up a custom domain](/portal/custom-domain) like `changelog.yourcompany.com`
- [Embed a What’s New panel](/embeds/widget) in your app
- [Publish from your terminal or CI](/cli/overview) with the CLI
