# Email Subscriptions

Readers can subscribe to your changelog with their email address. Subscribers are saved to your account, and you can push them to your email tool automatically with an [app](/apps/overview).

<Note>
  Collecting subscribers works on every plan. Connecting an app to sync them onward is available on the **Pro** plan and above.
</Note>

## Turning on the form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your subscription settings">
    Go to **Settings → Portal → Subscriptions**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the form">
    Switch on **Show subscription form on changelog**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your portal">
    A **Subscribe** button appears next to your portal's title. Clicking it opens a small form asking for an email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once someone subscribes, the button changes to **Subscribed** for them, so they aren't asked twice.

The same page has a **Show RSS subscription feed** toggle for the RSS button. The two are independent — you can offer either, both, or neither.

## Who we email

Nobody, for now. updates.page stores your subscribers and hands them to your email tool; it doesn't send the announcement emails itself.

That means the sequence is:

1. A reader subscribes on your portal.
2. We save them to your account.
3. If you've connected an app, we add them to your list there within seconds.
4. You send the broadcast from your email tool, when you're ready.

<Note>
  Sending changelog emails directly from updates.page is on the roadmap. Until then, connect an app so your list is ready to go.
</Note>

## Managing subscribers

**Settings → Portal → Subscribers** lists everyone who has signed up, newest first, with a search box for finding a specific address.

Each subscriber shows:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Email** | The address they gave |
| **Status** | `subscribed`, `unsubscribed`, or `bounced` |
| **Source** | Where they signed up — `portal` today |
| **Date** | When they subscribed |

**Export** downloads the whole list as a CSV — email, name, status, source, and dates. It's the same list whatever your plan, and it's how you move subscribers into an email tool you connected after they signed up (see [Apps](/apps/overview)).

Deleting a subscriber removes them from your account permanently. It does **not** remove them from the email tool you've synced them to — do that in the tool itself.

<Tip>
  Your subscriber count also shows on the **Reports** page, alongside views and posts.
</Tip>

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do subscribers have to confirm their address?">
    Not with updates.page. We save the address as soon as it's submitted.

    Some email tools run their own confirmation step when a contact is added, in which case your reader will get a confirmation email from that tool rather than from us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What stops spam sign-ups?">
    The form carries a hidden field that automated scripts fill in and people don't, and there's a rate limit on how many sign-ups one visitor can submit. Addresses that aren't valid email addresses are rejected.

    If junk does get through, delete it from the Subscribers page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if someone subscribes twice?">
    Nothing — the second attempt is accepted and quietly ignored. You'll never get duplicate rows for the same address, and casing doesn't matter: `Reader@Example.com` and `reader@example.com` are the same person.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I subscribe from my own preview?">
    No. The **Customize** page renders a live preview of your portal, and subscribing from it is blocked so you don't add yourself by accident. Use your real portal address to test it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to subscribers if I turn the form off?">
    They stay. Turning the toggle off hides the form and stops accepting new sign-ups; the people who already subscribed remain on your Subscribers page and keep syncing to a connected app if their status changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the RSS feed still work?">
    Yes, and it's independent of this. See [Portal Overview](/portal/overview) for your feed address.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
