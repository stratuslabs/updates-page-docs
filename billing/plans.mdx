## Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    Navigate to **Settings → Team → Billing**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click **Upgrade** on the plan you want
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Add your credit card information
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your upgrade takes effect immediately
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment methods

We accept:
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover

Payments are processed securely through Stripe.

## Billing cycle

- Plans are billed monthly or annually (annual is two months free)
- Upgrades take effect immediately
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period
- No contracts — cancel anytime
