# Billing FAQ

Answers to common billing questions.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade my plan?">
    Go to **Settings → Team → Billing**, click **Upgrade**, then **Select plan** on the plan you want. Your upgrade takes effect immediately, and you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing period.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Go to **Settings → Team → Billing** and click **Cancel subscription**. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period, then you'll be downgraded to the Free plan.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens when I downgrade?">
    When you downgrade:
    - Your posts and data are preserved
    - Features from higher tiers stop working, but nothing is deleted — your settings are still there if you upgrade again
    - Teammates over your new seat limit keep their access; you just can't invite anyone new until you're back under it
    - Paid features continue until the end of your billing period
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    We offer refunds within 14 days of your first payment if you're not satisfied. For refund requests, contact [hey@updates.page](mailto:hey@updates.page).
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Do you offer annual billing?">
    Yes — every paid plan has an annual option with two months free: Pro is $290/year and Business is $990/year. Switch between monthly and annual any time from **Settings → Team → Billing** → **Change plan**; unused time is credited automatically.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I change my billing email?">
    Yes. Go to **Settings → Team → Billing** and update your billing email. Receipts will be sent to the new address.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Where can I find my invoices?">
    Go to **Settings → Team → Billing** and scroll down to see your invoice history. Click any invoice to download a PDF.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens if my payment fails?">
    We'll retry the payment a few times. If it continues to fail, we'll email you to update your payment method. After 14 days of failed payments, your account may be downgraded.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or startups?">
    Yes! Contact [hey@updates.page](mailto:hey@updates.page) with details about your organization, and we'll see if you qualify for a discount.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    We don't offer a trial of paid plans, but the Free plan is free forever — start there and upgrade whenever you need more. New accounts do get 14 days of API and CLI access on the Free plan, so you can try publishing from your terminal or CI before you pick a plan. If a paid plan doesn't work out, refunds are available within 14 days of your first payment.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What's your privacy policy?">
    We take privacy seriously. Read our [Privacy Policy](https://updates.page/privacy) for details on how we handle your data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
