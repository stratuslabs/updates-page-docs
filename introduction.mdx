## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your changelog">
    Sign up and customize your portal with your brand colors and logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write announcements">
    Use our rich editor to write updates. Add images, videos, and code blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Hit publish and your changelog is live. Share the link or embed it in your app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
