# Team Members

Invite your team to collaborate on posts.

## Inviting members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Team → Members**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click the **Invite Member** button
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Enter their email address and select a role
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invite">
    They'll receive an email with a link to join
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing members

### Viewing members

Go to **Settings → Team → Members** to see all team members, their roles, and when they joined.

### Removing members

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click the **...** menu
3. Select **Remove from team**

The member will lose access immediately.

### Changing roles

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click the **...** menu
3. Select **Change role**
4. Choose the new role

See [Roles & Permissions](/team/roles) for role details.

## Pending invites

Invites expire after 7 days. To resend:

1. Go to **Settings → Team → Members**
2. Find the pending invite
3. Click **Resend invite**

To cancel a pending invite, click **Revoke**.
