# Team Members

Invite your team to collaborate on posts. Only the account owner can manage members.

## Inviting members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Team → Members**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click the **Invite Member** button
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Invited teammates join as [members](/team/roles)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invite">
    They'll receive an email with a link to join
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seat limits

Each plan includes a number of seats:

| Plan | Included Seats |
|------|----------------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 1 |
| Business | 10 |

Pending invites count against your seats until they're accepted or removed.

<Note>
  Need more than 10 seats? [Contact us](mailto:hey@updates.page) for a custom plan.
</Note>

## Managing members

Go to **Settings → Team → Members** to see everyone on the account, including pending invites.

- **Resend invite** — for someone who hasn't accepted yet
- **Remove** — revokes access immediately, and works for pending invites too

<Note>
  Removing someone keeps their posts. Anything they wrote is reassigned to the account owner so nothing on your changelog disappears.
</Note>

## Publish notifications

When a post is published, everyone else on the account gets an email letting them know. Each person can turn that off for themselves in **Settings → Account → Notifications**.

Imported posts never trigger these emails, so [importing an archive](/posts/importing) won't flood your team's inbox.
