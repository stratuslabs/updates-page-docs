# Importing Posts

Moving from another changelog tool? Import your existing posts from its RSS or Atom feed — Headway, Beamer, Canny, and most blogs expose one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your feed URL">
    Usually something like `https://yourcompany.headwayapp.co/changelog.rss`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the import">
    Go to **Settings → Changelog → Import**, paste the feed URL, and start it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait">
    The import runs in the background. Larger feeds with lots of images can take a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets imported

- Up to **200 posts** per feed, published with their original dates
- **Categories** from the feed, created on your account as needed
- **Images**, re-hosted on updates.page so they don't break when the old tool goes away

Imported posts do **not** email your team, so bringing over a large archive won't send a wave of notifications.

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Running an import twice is safe">
    Posts already imported from the same feed are skipped, so you can re-run an import to pick up what's new.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One import at a time">
    Wait for the current import to finish before starting another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only the account owner can import">
    Members don't see the Import page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some images may keep their old URLs">
    Very image-heavy feeds hit a re-hosting budget. Those images still work, but they're served from the original host — swap them out if you plan to shut it down.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Imported posts are ordinary posts. Edit, recategorize, or delete them like anything else.
</Tip>
