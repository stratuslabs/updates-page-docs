# Scheduling Posts

Plan ahead by scheduling posts to publish automatically at a future date and time.

## How to schedule a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your post">
    Write your post as usual in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    The publish dialog opens with **Now** selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Later">
    Pick the date and time you want the post to go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Schedule">
    Your post publishes automatically at that time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing scheduled posts

Scheduled posts appear in your posts list with a **Scheduled** badge.

You can:
- **Edit** the content before it publishes
- **Reschedule** — reopen the publish dialog and pick a new time
- **Publish now** — reopen the dialog and choose **Now**
- **Delete** to cancel the scheduled post

## Timezone handling

Scheduled times use your computer's timezone — the time you pick is the time you see. Behind the scenes it's stored as an exact moment, so a teammate in another timezone sees the same post publish at the same instant.

<Tip>
  Scheduling from the [CLI](/cli/overview)? `--at` takes ISO 8601, and a value without a timezone is read as local time.
</Tip>
